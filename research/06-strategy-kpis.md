# Market entry, operating KPIs, and risk

## A staged decision

| Stage | Evidence required | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Desk research | Product definition, reviewed sources, regulatory path, supplier shortlist | Decide which product/channel hypothesis deserves a quote and buyer interview |
| Feasibility | Written ingredient/equipment/contract-production quotes, measured line trials, shelf-life plan | Decide whether a paid pilot can be delivered within budget |
| Paid pilot | Predeclared contribution and repeat-order endpoints, complete delivery and quality data | Expand, redesign, or stop based on measured outcomes |
| Territory scale | Repeat demand sufficient for fixed costs, fulfillment reliability, funding and working capital | Add accounts/capacity only after demand and operational constraints are reconciled |

Numeric commercial gates should be set from quotes and investor/operator constraints before the pilot. The illustrative model's break-even values are a starting calculation, not an approved target.

## KPI dictionary

| KPI | Numerator / denominator | Cadence and accountable role | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realized net price | invoiced revenue less discounts, rebates, returns / sold good kg | Monthly, finance | Compare price to actual cost after channel deductions |
| Contribution per kg | net revenue less explicitly defined variable costs / sold good kg | Weekly/monthly, finance | Detect unprofitable volume growth |
| 60-day paid reorder | accounts with second paid delivery within 60 days / first-order accounts with full 60-day window | Monthly cohort, commercial | Separate trial from repeat demand |
| Saleable throughput | released good kg / scheduled production hours | Per run, operations | Include downtime and quality losses |
| Availability | running hours / scheduled hours | Per shift, operations | Target cleaning, maintenance, and changeover losses |
| Performance | actual running kg / rated kg at actual running hours | Per run, engineering | Identify speed losses using validated reference speed |
| First-pass quality yield | good released kg / produced kg | Per batch, quality | Track scrap and rework with a defined policy |
| On-time in-full delivery | complete orders on promised date / eligible orders | Weekly, supply chain | Guard against demand exceeding service capacity |
| Spoilage | expired/damaged kg / available kg | Weekly, supply chain | Evaluate shelf-life and forecast error |
| Customer concentration | largest account's net revenue / total net revenue | Monthly, finance | Avoid scaling around one uncontracted buyer |
| Cash conversion cycle | inventory days + receivable days − payable days | Monthly, finance | Forecast funding needs |

Assign local owners for a real pilot; roles above are proposed accountabilities. Do not optimize a single metric without guardrails: speed gains must not reduce quality or safety; conversion gains must not destroy contribution.

## Risk register

| Risk | Early signal | Mitigation or validation | Residual uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taste does not produce repeat demand | High trial, low paid reorder | Blind sensory study plus account cohorts | Sampling and channel bias |
| Capacity claim overstates saleable output | High cleaning time, low release yield | Witnessed full-shift line trial and mass balance | Scale-up and operator learning |
| Premium price reduces demand | Strong intent, low paid conversion | Randomized offers and contribution endpoint | Price/adoption correlation |
| Supplier lock-in | Premix/printer dependency, single-source parts | Quote alternative supply, service SLA, switching cost | Recipe and intellectual-property constraints |
| Regulatory change | New bill, order, labeling requirement | Product-specific counsel review before launch | Court and legislative timing |
| Working-capital shortfall | Long buyer terms and frozen stock | Monthly cash forecast and staged commitments | Returns and collection risk |
| Sustainability claim fails boundary test | Missing energy/yield/ingredient data | Comparative independently reviewed LCA | Geographic/process sensitivity |
| Concentrated demand | One account dominates utilization | Contract coverage and diversified buyer pilot | Reorder uncertainty |

## Alternative comparison

Before selecting a printer, compare buying finished goods, contract production, licensing, extrusion/molding, and owning a printing line. Use an identical functional output (saleable kg meeting the same sensory specification), demand path, channel, and cash horizon. Include minimum order quantities, changeovers, validation, freight, waste, and service obligations. A technologically distinctive process is not automatically the economically preferred process.
