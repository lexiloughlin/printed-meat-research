# Evidence audit and research limitations

## Evidence classes

**Reviewed primary:** source read during this task; scope and limitations recorded. **Company reported:** useful for product specifications and announcements, with commercial incentives and no independent performance validation. **Legacy unverified:** inherited source not fully checked during this review. **Analyst assumption:** explicit scenario parameter. **Derived:** reproducible calculation linked to its inputs.

Do not collapse these classes into a single numerical “confidence” score. A source may establish one narrow fact strongly and support no broader inference.

## Survey interpretation

Bitkom describes a telephone survey of 1,004 German residents aged 16+, fielded during calendar weeks 7–10 of 2025. The 24% value is openness to cultivated printed meat. The reported increase from 13% is 11 percentage points, not 11 percent; the relative arithmetic increase is about 85%, but questionnaire and sample comparability should be checked before treating it as a robust trend. The 7% premium willingness figure is a separate marginal response and cannot be divided by 24% to infer willingness among open respondents without joint data.

Age groups are unequal in width and population size. Do not average their percentages to reconstruct the national result, infer causality from age, pool respondents across survey rows, or use total n for each subgroup. The source describes representativeness, but full weighting, response rate, and design effect are not available here. A simple binomial confidence interval would obscure those gaps.

## Market-report interpretation

The original $178.64bn (2023) and $2.92bn (2024) claims differ in geography/scope definitions and base year. Their ratio does not isolate methodology error. Arithmetic CAGR can be recomputed from endpoints, but consistency does not establish valid market coverage. Retain the figures as a research-quality case study; do not average them, use them as lower/upper estimates, or multiply them by a generic market share to forecast printed-meat revenue.

A separate US plant-based meat/seafood retail benchmark [E02] includes many products and manufacturing methods. It is contextual category evidence, not printed-product market size. It cannot be directly compared with a global equipment-plus-food estimate.

## Regulatory interpretation

Reviewed federal pages distinguish FDA's cell-culture role from FSIS harvest/processing/labeling oversight for relevant species. The consultation inventory lists completed consultations and supporting materials, not blanket approvals for every process. Texas SB 261 establishes a sales/offer-for-sale restriction for defined cell-cultured protein and a scheduled September 1, 2027 expiry. The text alone does not establish current litigation status. The legacy statement about production bans and daily penalties needs separate enforcement-law review; it is not repeated as a verified conclusion here.

Plant-derived ingredients do not trigger this particular cell-cultured definition merely because a printer is used. That does not exempt a food from general safety, ingredient, labeling, allergen, or other applicable rules. Build a product/species/jurisdiction matrix for a real launch.

## Scientific and environmental limits

The legacy technical paper's full page was blocked by an automated access challenge during this review. Detailed recipe and infill conclusions remain unverified here. Manufacturing comparisons must cover formulation, sensory design, and cooking. Environmental performance needs a life-cycle boundary, functional unit, geography-specific energy mix, ingredient production, yield, cold chain, and disposal assumptions. No universal emissions reduction or nutrition superiority is asserted.

## Reproducibility and disclosure

Record source URL, exact supported claim, evidence type, review date, limitations, and intended use. Version inputs and results together. Do not silently overwrite a changing web page with an implied historic observation. The expanded model is independent of the legacy market and capacity claims. No private sales records, supplier quotes, buyer interviews, or experimental results were available.
