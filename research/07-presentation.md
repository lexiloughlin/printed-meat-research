# Ten-minute portfolio presentation

Use this as a speaking outline or convert sections into slides. Every financial exhibit must say **illustrative assumptions**.

| Time | Exhibit / message | Evidence and talking point |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | Research question | Can printed whole cuts earn repeat demand and sustainable contribution? |
| 0:45–1:45 | Define the category | Separate ingredient biology from the manufacturing process; show the technology table |
| 1:45–2:45 | Evidence quality | Explain why German cultivated-product openness cannot calibrate US plant-based sales |
| 2:45–3:45 | Competitive business models | Compare branded food, equipment/consumables, and licensing; distinguish announcements from sales |
| 3:45–5:00 | Demand meets capacity | Explain min(demand, saleable capacity), channel deductions, yield, and bottlenecks |
| 5:00–6:00 | Scenario operating results | Show the generated scenario figure and exact table; cases are hypothetical |

| 8:00–9:15 | Proposed pilot | Blind tasting, randomized offers, and mature 60-day reorder cohorts |
| 9:15–10:00 | Decision and next evidence | Obtain quotes and paid reorder data before committing to owned capacity |

## Questions to prepare for

**“What is the market size?”** No defensible printed-meat TAM is established here. The account model sizes a hypothetical territory; the historical publisher estimates have different scopes and are excluded from calibration.

**“Is your simulated probability real?”** It is the share of draws exceeding a threshold under chosen independent triangular distributions. It is not calibrated against commercial outcomes.

**“Why foodservice?”** It is a proposed testable channel with identifiable buyers and repeated orders. Relative channel attractiveness still requires quotes and pilot evidence.

**“What makes the project advanced?”** It reconciles market evidence, capacity, unit economics, cash timing, uncertainty, experimental design, and reproducibility. The sophistication is in controlling assumptions and decisions, not adding unsupported precision.

**“What did you personally contribute?”** Describe only the work you reviewed, reproduced, and understand. This expansion was AI-assisted; independently verify material claims and be ready to explain each formula and limitation.
