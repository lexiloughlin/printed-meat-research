# Prioritized research backlog

| Priority | Question | Evidence to obtain | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Is the exact product lawful in the chosen market today? | Current product/species review, statutes, rules, court orders | Dated jurisdiction checklist with primary links and qualified review |
| P0 | What does a buyer actually pay? | Written distributor/restaurant quotes including deductions and returns | Net price reconciliation by SKU, currency, pack, and date |
| P0 | Is repeat demand sufficient? | Consented paid-pilot deliveries with mature follow-up | Cohort denominator, repeat quantity, and contribution reproducible |
| P0 | What is achievable saleable output? | Full-shift production logs including cleaning and rejected mass | Reconciled time and mass balance across repeated runs |
| P1 | What is true installed cost? | Multiple supplier/contract-manufacturer quotes | Installation, facilities, service, validation, and contingency included |
| P1 | Does structuring add value over alternatives? | Matched recipe/cooking comparison and blinded sensory test | Predeclared endpoint, effect size, uncertainty, and protocol deviations |
| P1 | What is the cash downside? | Contract payment terms, inventory policy, shelf-life validation | Monthly working-capital forecast with stress cases |
| P1 | Are competitor milestones current? | Official releases, product listings, filings | Plan/actual/status separated with dates; no unsupported “latest” label |
| P2 | What are full environmental impacts? | Product/process-specific comparative LCA | Consistent functional unit and boundary; sensitivity to energy and yield |
| P2 | Can the territory estimate scale? | Verified eligible-account frame and observed channel behavior | Deduplicated universe, serviceability evidence, and bounded extrapolation |

Recommended next collection is a small set of rigorous quotes and paid observations rather than additional unsourced forecast reports. Keep a revision log showing which assumptions changed and whether the commercial decision changed.
